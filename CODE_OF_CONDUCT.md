# Code of conduct

We want VietaMath to be a useful and welcoming project.

Be respectful. Keep discussions about the work, not the person. Give people room to ask
basic questions and to correct mistakes. Do not harass, threaten, insult, or
discriminate against other contributors.

Maintainers may edit or remove material that breaks these rules and may limit a
person's participation when needed. Serious or private concerns can be reported
through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
